About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The system aims to provide real-time heatwave monitoring and early warning so that disaster-management teams and citizens can respond quickly to extreme heat events.

Data Sources

Data is collected from AWS sensors, the IMD database, and satellite feeds to ensure accurate and timely forecasting.

Stakeholders

Key stakeholders include meteorologists, disaster-management officers, and the general public, all of whom rely on this portal for heatwave-related decisions.

"Timely and accurate heatwave alerts save lives. Our goal is to make this information accessible to everyone, everywhere."
- Chief Meteorologist, Heatwave Intelligence System
